Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Clips AI Uploader is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's computer.

  • What it accesses: basic TikTok profile info (open ID) and permission to upload and post videos, granted through TikTok Login.
  • Where data is kept: access tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner's computer. The tool has no server and no analytics.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, when the user starts an upload.
  • Deletion: revoking access in TikTok's settings and deleting the local token file removes all access.
